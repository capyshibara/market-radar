## Event-first staged curation replaces the arbitrary 180-document stop (2026-08-06)

This checkpoint answers the operator's explicit challenge: there was no evidence that the 230
documents behind the old 180-document Researcher cutoff contained less marginal value. The cutoff
has been removed as a termination rule. This implementation made **no paid API call and no write
to the production database on port 8081**; runtime validation used an isolated H2 in-memory app on
port 18083.

### Researcher: bounded actions, complete corpus obligation

- `ResearchCurationPlanner` deterministically builds conservative story clusters over **every**
  eligible confirmed document. It uses compatible legal entity, market, topic and a 14-day date
  window, then requires high title/content similarity. Ambiguity fails open as a separate cluster;
  complete-link grouping prevents a loose A≈B≈C chain from collapsing two different stories.
- A main action selects at most 20 unrepresented clusters, with up to two different publishers per
  cluster. Missing topic, acquisition lane, Vietnam coverage, primary authority and legal entity
  are seeded before score. Later actions continue into unrepresented clusters; there is no
  permanent `top 180` set.
- Extra members of represented clusters enter a deterministic stratified audit pool. Analyst may
  not start until every conservative cluster has a successful current extraction and the deferred
  audit pool is exhausted. The emergency ceiling of 500 is only a spend firebreak above the
  current corpus; reaching it produces `OPERATOR_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, never a low-value judgement.
- Each paid main/audit action is recorded in `research_curation_batches`, including selected
  documents/clusters and the delta in normalized event clusters, corroborations and conflicts.
  This creates the marginal-value evidence that the old fixed cutoff lacked.
- `EMPTY_RESULT` and `SCHEMA_REJECTED` do not create an infinite automatic retry loop and do not
  count as represented. Their exhausted cluster is held for operator inspection/targeted retry.
  Provider/LLM errors remain retryable because they may be transient.

### Analyst: 180 is no longer a hidden downstream cutoff

- The separate Analyst `max-documents: 180` cutoff was also retired. The new
  `marketradar.analyst.batch-documents: 60` is an operational batch size only. Each run excludes
  documents with a current interpretation edition and automatically continues into the eligible
  tail on the next run.
- Checkpoint and BI publication eligibility use the complete eligible evidence map, not the
  current 60-document action. Corpus-wide executive/chapter/deep-dive synthesis is withheld until
  the final document batch is complete, preventing polished analysis of partial coverage.
- Therefore both expensive stages now separate **cost per action** from **evidence completion**:
  the batch size controls spend and recoverability; cluster coverage + deferred audit determine
  when the pipeline may advance.

### Operator control and exact next run

1. Rebuild/restart the app; the currently running old JAR cannot expose these changes.
2. Open `/pipeline/researcher-curation`. This read-only page makes no model call. It shows eligible
   documents, conservative clusters, represented clusters, the remaining audit pool, coverage
   gaps, next paid batch, deferred audit preview and batch value ledger in EN/VI.
3. Run **Researcher + Connector** one bounded batch at a time. Reopen the page after each batch.
   Follow its recommendation: `Run next evidence batch`, then `Audit deferred evidence`, until it

4. Run **Analyst + Fact-checker** repeatedly. Each action advances to the next unrepresented

5. Only then run Independent Verifier and Human Editor. No step above requires Anthropic Deep

| Policy | Paid classify/extract/analyse | Highest publication lane |
|---|---:|---|

## Downstream quality audit completed in code (2026-08-05, before the clean run)

This checkpoint deliberately **did not run Scout or any AI stage and did not mutate the production
database**. The previous port-8081 process was stopped so the release suite could enforce that
boundary. All runtime smoke tests used a new H2 database under `/private/tmp`; the operator can now

### One operating model, with reusable intelligence dimensions

The implementation now follows the CFO's sequence without jumping from collection to analysis:

**Scout → Librarian + Router → Researcher + Connector → Analyst + Fact-checker (Gate L1) →
Independent Verifier (Gate L2) → Human Editor**.

Every item carries independent dimensions instead of overloading one legacy `tier`: source
authority, market scope/code, resolved legal entity, intelligence topic, temporal role, target
department, corroboration and publication grade. This lets the same evidence corpus support
Vietnam Strategy now, an international-market view later, and Product/Sales/Compliance policies
without copying the crawler or treating language, publisher headquarters or URL suffix as market
evidence.

### Stage-by-stage firebreaks and fixes

- **Scout / source boundary:** source credibility (`REGULATOR`, `STATUTORY_DISCLOSURE`, official
  company, industry body, specialist research, established media, etc.) is separate from geography.
  Known sources receive explicit metadata; operator activation/health decisions survive restart.
  Browser-only success remains manual evidence and cannot silently certify an automatic parser.
  Language, `.vn` and old tier values no longer fabricate event geography.
- **Librarian + Router:** classification now receives a representative long-document excerpt plus
  source authority, market and time context. Strategy and department routing share a broader topic
  taxonomy. Long research documents are not reduced to the opening paragraph, while incomplete,
  duplicate and sample documents remain auditable without consuming an LLM call.
- **Dedup:** exact URL/hash and near-identical content can be `DUPLICATE_CONTENT`; an independently
  published report about the same event is `SAME_EVENT_INDEPENDENT` and is retained for
  corroboration. Similar titles alone never delete a source. Report queries exclude only actual
  duplicate/sample records.
- **Researcher + Connector:** extraction uses bounded overlapping chunks and persists exact spans.
  Market, topic, time and legal-entity metadata are derived before synthesis. One document failing
  extraction cannot roll back successful documents from the same run.
- **Entity attribution:** legal entities sharing a brand are distinct (`Prudential Việt Nam`,
  `Prudential plc`, and US `Prudential Financial/PGIM`). A headline or model-proposed company can
  never override the exact evidence span. Bare brands stay ambiguous unless a concrete country
  context resolves them; `GLOBAL`, `REGIONAL` and `MULTI` are coverage labels, not jurisdictions.
- **Connector / clustering:** clusters require compatible market, month, topic and resolved
  entity/product or named KPI. This prevents unrelated stories about one insurer being merged,
  while preserving legitimate multi-publisher corroboration of the same result/event.
- **Analyst + Gate L1:** observation and implication are separate fields. Occurred, effective,
  expiry and forecast dates are separate; the report window uses the appropriate semantic date,
  not whichever date happens to be available. Every interpreted claim is saved independently so a
  later failure cannot erase earlier successful work.
- **Independent Verifier + publication:** verification is persisted claim-by-claim in an isolated
  transaction. An entity-attribution warning downgrades rather than passes. Publication has three
  explicit lanes: `DECISION_GRADE` (approved + latest ENTAILED + attribution safe),
  `REVIEWED_ANALYSIS` (human-approved NEUTRAL analytical material), and `EDITORIAL_WATCH`
  (human-approved NEUTRAL observation). Contradicted, technically failed, stale, partially cited,
  duplicate/sample or entity-unsafe material is excluded. Analysis/watch material cannot drive
  executive assertions or charts.
- **Human Editor / reports:** BI pages prioritize source-backed developments and visibly separate
  decision-grade conclusions, reviewed analysis and watch items. Authority, market and intake
  method remain visible in citations. Product-department editorial drafts, when that optional desk
  is enabled, are now bound to a fingerprint of the current eligible evidence ledger. Static
  showcase copy is an unsigned template only; it cannot appear publicly until an SME checks the
  locked current fact codes, cites them in every takeaway/exhibit and signs the draft. New evidence
  automatically makes an old draft stale instead of silently carrying its claims forward.

## Việc còn mở — ưu tiên cho phiên sau

1. **[Cao nhất] Xác nhận bản xem nhanh PPTX/PDF đã hết lỗi "Tổng hợp AI không tạo được nhận
   định có cấu trúc" chưa.** Đã yêu cầu người dùng dán log đầy đủ của lượt chạy mới nhất
   (`deepresearch176`, 04/08 16:59) nhưng **chưa nhận được** khi phiên này kết thúc. Nếu log cho
   thấy vẫn là JSON bị cắt do Max tokens: cân nhắc sửa tận gốc thay vì chỉ hướng dẫn người dùng tự
   tăng — ví dụ tách `synthesize()` dùng 1 override max_tokens riêng cao hơn (không phụ thuộc
   Max tokens cấu hình chung của Writer, vốn dùng cho cả Extract/Interpret theo-từng-fact ít tốn
   token hơn nhiều), hoặc chia nhỏ lệnh gọi tổng hợp theo bucket thay vì 1 lệnh duy nhất cho tất
   cả 25 nguồn.
2. **Xác nhận bug gzip (2.9) đã hết rác thật** — yêu cầu 1 lượt Deep Research mới, đọc lại file
   PDF/PPTX xuất ra, xác nhận không còn đoạn `##...` nào.
3. **Default `Max tokens = 1024` ở `/llm-settings` cho Writer có thể vẫn là bẫy cho người dùng
   mới** — hiện chỉ có cảnh báo SAU KHI lỗi xảy ra (đúng thiết kế fail-loud), nhưng nếu muốn chủ
   động hơn có thể nâng default hoặc thêm ghi chú ngay trên form.
4. **Chưa có ai chạy pipeline thật để nhìn báo cáo BI kỳ mới với đủ 8 bucket + trang DEEP_DIVE**
   (Phần 1) — cần ít nhất 1 lượt Classify→Extract→Interpret→Publish thật rồi mở `/report/bi` xem
   trực quan, hiện chỉ mới xác nhận bằng test logic đứng riêng.
5. **3 test standalone của Phần 1 chưa vào `src/test`** — nếu team muốn giữ làm regression, copy
   `RouterApplyTest`/`ConnectorTest`/`SanitizeKeyTest` từ scratchpad vào cây test chính (nội dung
   cụ thể không còn trong repo này — cần viết lại nếu cần, logic đã mô tả ở Phần 1).
6. **`br` (Brotli) content-encoding chưa giải được** (2.9) — nếu sau này gặp nguồn thật trả về
   Brotli (không hiếm với Cloudflare), `SafeFetcher` sẽ từ chối rõ ràng thay vì đọc rác (đúng
   thiết kế fail-loud), nhưng nguồn đó sẽ bị bỏ qua hoàn toàn. Nếu tần suất gặp cao, cân nhắc thêm
   thư viện Brotli decode (chưa có trong `pom.xml`).
